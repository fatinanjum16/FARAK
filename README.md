# FARAK — Scanimation Art Generator

Turn any photos or videos into scanimation art.

**Live:** https://fatinanjum16.github.io/FARAK/

## What it does

- Pick a short video (up to 3 seconds) or 1–8 photos.
- FARAK builds the striped scanimation image and the matching overlay grid, and previews the animation.
- Download both images (`farak.png` and `farak-grid.png`).
- Everything runs in your browser. Your photos and videos are never uploaded.
